Chemie Lernen — the free, ad-free chemistry app for students and everyone who wants to understand chemistry.

The app presents the content-rich learning platform chemie-lernen.org in a mobile-optimized way — no subscription, no ads, no tracking.

📚 12 chemistry topics
From introduction to chemistry over atomic structure, acids & bases, electrochemistry & redox to biochemistry — structured content for school and university.

🧮 10+ chemistry calculators
• Molar mass calculator
• pH calculator (pH/pOH, strong & weak acids)
• Stoichiometry & balancing chemical equations
• Gas laws
• Concentration & dilution
• Density, solubility, combustion, redox potential

🎬 15 educational videos
High-quality learning videos by Zig's Chemistry 42 (Prof. Siegfried Schindler, JLU Giessen), embedded in matching topics.

🕸️ Chemistry knowledge graph
An interactive knowledge network that visualizes the connections between the core concepts of chemistry — perfect for reviewing and connecting ideas.

Features:
• Native Android app (Jetpack Compose, Material 3)
• Fast navigation via bottom navigation bar
• Offline-capable through WebView cache
• No sign-up, no account data, no permissions besides Internet
• No ads, no tracking, no data sharing
• Open source (Apache-2.0)

All content is provided by chemie-lernen.org (Tobias Weiß) and is continuously updated. The app is an open-source wrapper that shows the website mobile-optimized.

Ideal for: high school chemistry, chemistry studies, exam preparation, general chemistry, inorganic chemistry, organic chemistry, physical chemistry.
